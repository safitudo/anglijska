# 1. HOP ON, HOP OFF

**Про що:** Варшава — туристичний автобус, їжа на кожній зупинці, ввечері пиво і коньяк.

**Bonus words:** Warsaw (Варшава), hop on / hop off (заскочив / вискочив), cognac (коньяк)

**Style:**
```
Upbeat Polish polka ska with accordion and brass band, joyful male choir, oompah rhythm, comedic festive energy
```

**Lyrics:**
```
[Intro]
(spoken) Варшава!

[Verse 1]
We are in Warsaw! Hello, Warsaw!
The big bus goes in the city.
We have two tickets — you and me!
Hop on! Hop off! Come with me!

[Chorus]
HOP ON! HOP OFF! (hey!)
HOP ON! HOP OFF! (hop!)
We see the city from the bus!
The old street! The new street! The church! The park!
HOP ON! HOP OFF! (hey!)

[Verse 2]
We hop off. Why? Food!
We eat and eat and eat and eat.
We hop on. Bus! Go, go!
We hop off. Why? ...Food again!

[Verse 3]
My son says: again?!
I say: yes! We are in Warsaw!
Breakfast two! Lunch three!
This city is very good to me!

[Chorus]

[Verse 4] (slower, evening)
In the night — a small beer. One.
Okay, two. Okay... three.
And after beer — a small cognac.
(spoken, toast) Ну, за Варшаву!

[Bridge] (tired oompah, morning after)
Morning. I am tired. Very tired.
But the bus waits! The bus says: come!
Hop on... hop off... slowly... slowly...
Okay, bus. Okay. We come.

[Outro]
Goodbye, Warsaw! Thank you, bus!
Now — the airport! Now — the plane!
We go to Greece! To the sea!
(spoken) А коньяк був добрий!
```
