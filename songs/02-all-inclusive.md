# 2. ALL INCLUSIVE

**Про що:** готель «все включено» — перемога над системою. Кавун щодня. Алкоголь поганий... але безкоштовний.

**Bonus words:** all inclusive (все включено), beer (пиво), free (безкоштовно), watermelon (кавун)

**Style:**
```
Triumphant stadium rock anthem, big male vocals, crowd chants, arena drums, celebratory comedic energy
```

**Lyrics:**
```
[Verse 1]
Morning in the hotel. I go to breakfast.
I eat bread. I eat fruit. I eat... again.
The man says: coffee? I say: yes!
And after coffee — breakfast two!

[Chorus]
ALL INCLUSIVE! I don't pay!
ALL INCLUSIVE! I eat all day!
Food and coffee! Fruit and tea!
This hotel is good to me!

[Verse 2]
Day one: watermelon. Day two: watermelon.
Day three? ...Watermelon!
It is big, it is cold, it is very good.
Watermelon — my new friend!

[Verse 3] (skeptical)
The free beer is... hmm. Not good.
The free cognac is... oh no. No-no.
But how much is it? I DON'T PAY!
(spoken, sips) Ну... безкоштовно ж.
Free bad beer... is okay beer!

[Chorus]

[Verse 4]
My room is good. The window is big.
"You see the sea!" ...I see the street.
I see a car. Where is the sea?
I don't know. But breakfast is at seven!

[Bridge]
Dinner one... dinner two... dinner three...
I go to bed. I sleep. And in my sleep — I eat!
Tomorrow: breakfast at seven.
I know. I wait. I am here.

[Outro]
Goodbye, hotel... my good friend...
I don't want to leave! I want to stay!
ALL INCLUSIVE! One week more!
(spoken) Ой, як добре...
```
