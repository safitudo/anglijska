# 3. EXPENSIVE

**Жарт:** чоловік добре поїв у ресторані, а потім побачив рахунок. І чайові. Трагедія в трьох діях.

**Style:**
```
Slow mournful country blues ballad, sad twangy guitar, deep melodramatic male voice almost crying, whiskey-bar atmosphere, comedic melodrama
```

**Lyrics:**
```
[Verse 1]
Night. City. Restaurant.
Good table. Big menu.
I want meat. I want fish. I want salad.
I eat. I drink. I eat again.
Happy man... happy, happy man.

[Verse 2]
I say: bill, please.
Restaurant man come. He have bill.
I see bill...
I see bill...
HOW MUCH?!

[Chorus]
Expensive! Too much! EXPENSIVE!
Two hundred?! WHY?! WHY?!
Goodbye, money... goodbye, my money...
I want cheap. I need cheap. Where cheap?!

[Verse 3]
Salad — twenty. Bread — ten.
Water — ten?! WATER?! WHY?!
And what this? "Tip"?
Tip?! No, no, no, no, no.
(small voice) ...Okay. Tip.

[Chorus]

[Bridge]
Man ask: cash or card?
No cash... no card... no money...
Okay. I work here now.
One day. Two days. One week!

[Outro] (slow, very sad)
Tomorrow I eat in house.
Bread and water... bread and water...
Very cheap. Very good.
...I want meat.
```
