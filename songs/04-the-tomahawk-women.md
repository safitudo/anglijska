# 4. THE TOMAHAWK WOMEN ⭐

**Про що:** файний ресторан біля води в Нафпліо. За спиною сина — дві жінки і ЦІЛИЙ томагавк. Він говорив про це весь вечір.

**Bonus words:** tomahawk (томагавк), women (жінки — множина від woman), behind (позаду)

**Style:**
```
Dramatic Argentine tango, passionate theatrical male vocals, accordion and violin, comedic intensity with dramatic pauses
```

**Lyrics:**
```
[Verse 1]
Night in Nafplio. A good restaurant.
We are at the water. The fish is good.
But behind my son I see... I see...
Two women. And one BIG meat.

[Chorus] (dramatic hit)
TO-MA-HAWK! The meat is big!
Two women! One tomahawk!
How?! How can they eat it?!
I see... I don't know... I SEE!

[Verse 2]
I eat my fish. But I see them.
My son says: eat! I say: yes, yes...
But the meat is big — big as a house!
And they eat. And eat. They don't stop!

[Verse 3]
They are small! The meat is big!
Where does it go?! I don't know!
I drink my beer. I wait. I see.
And now — no meat. No meat. ALL.
(spoken, quiet) Оце так жінки...

[Chorus]

[Bridge] (slow tango)
All the night I say: how?
How? How? HOW?
My son says: sleep! I say: NO!
Two women... one tomahawk... HOW?!

[Outro]
Today. Tomorrow. One week after.
I say it again: two women. One tomahawk.
(spoken) Як?! Як вони його з'їли?!
I don't know... I don't know...
TO-MA-HAWK!
```
