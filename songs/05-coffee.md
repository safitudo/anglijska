# 5. COFFEE

**Жарт:** вранці без кави він не людина — навіть імені свого не знає. Госпел-хор молиться за каву. Після чотирьох чашок — нова людина.

**Style:**
```
Soulful gospel with full church choir, call and response, hammond organ, hand claps, passionate male lead building from weak whisper to triumphant, comedic reverence
```

**Lyrics:**
```
[Intro] (organ, slow, weak voice)
Morning... oh, morning...

[Verse 1]
Morning. I no man.
I walk slowly... I no see... I no know...
What my name?... I not know my name!
(he no know name!)

[Chorus]
COFFEE! (coffee!)
I NEED COFFEE! (he need coffee!)
Big coffee! Hot coffee! Two coffee!
COFFEE, PLEASE! (please! please!)

[Verse 2]
Woman say: hello! I say: ...coffee.
Child say: hello, hello! I say: ...coffee.
Phone say: ring-ring! I say: NO. Coffee first.
No coffee — no man. (no man!)

[Bridge] (building)
One coffee... I see! (he see!)
Two coffee... I walk! (he walk!)
Three coffee... I KNOW MY NAME! (he know name!)
Four coffee... I NEW MAN! (NEW MAN!)

[Chorus] (huge, full choir)

[Outro] (slow, warm)
Coffee... my friend. (friend...)
Good morning. Very good morning.
Tomorrow?... Again. (again! again!)
Amen. ...And coffee.
```
